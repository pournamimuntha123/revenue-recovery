   The first issue I hit was a crash while calculating total recovered amount.
   Data loaded from CSV comes in as text, and I was trying to add up amount
   fields without converting them to numbers first. I fixed it by explicitly
   converting each amount to a float when building the audit entry.

   The second challenge was making sure the system wouldn't act recklessly on
   unclear cases. I built a stress test with deliberately tricky records — a
   customer who'd already been retried 5 times, and payments with no failure
   reason at all — to confirm my stopping rules would catch these and escalate
   to a human instead of guessing or retrying endlessly. All 5 edge cases
   passed, which gave me confidence the system is genuinely bounded, not just
   working by luck on clean data.
