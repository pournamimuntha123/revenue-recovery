1. PROBLEM (30 sec)
- Failed payments and abandoned checkouts are a silent revenue leak for merchants
- Most of them are recoverable if you retry at the right time or nudge the customer correctly

2. HOW IT WORKS (60 sec)
- Flow: event happens -> system classifies why -> decides the right action -> applies safety stopping rules -> logs everything
- Clear cases use fast rule-based logic; only unclear cases get sent to an AI model for judgment

3. LIVE DEMO (2 min)
- Run py main.py on screen, show the report
- Point at recovery rate % and total amount recovered
- Open audit_log.csv, show TXN1006 (expired card, correctly did not retry) and TXN1008 (bank declined, correctly escalated to a human)

4. WHAT BROKE (60 sec)
- Hit a crash converting CSV text into numbers, fixed by converting amount fields to float
- Built a stress test with tricky edge cases (5 retries already, missing reason) to prove the safety rules actually hold, not just look good on clean data

5. WHAT'S NEXT (30 sec)
- Connect to real Razorpay APIs instead of simulated outcomes
- Expand AI classification to handle more ambiguous cases
